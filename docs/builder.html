Four ways to build, one language

Windows illustrations, illustrations pieced together from library assets, product UI illustrations, and any of those put inside a device. The four modes are different ways in, not four tools: they share the geometry, the single stroke, the thirteen color slots and the checks, which is what makes their output belong to one family.
