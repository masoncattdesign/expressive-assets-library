Distinctly Windows, Uniquely Mine. The surfaces and widgets the desktops are built from, in code, in the six themes from the Surfaces chart in Figma. The structure of each one never changes; the theme only swaps materials, color, corner shape and type. Press 1 to 6 for a theme, or 0 to compare all six.

Surfaces

Desktop, Start, Taskbar, the Universal Composer and four everyday apps, each at the size of its Figma component.

Widgets

Every widget in every size it supports. They are the same widgets Workbench and Mosaic use; here they take the theme's surfaces, ink, accent and corners.
