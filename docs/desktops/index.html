Desktops

Three working prototypes of what a desktop could feel like. Each one is built from its Figma frame and everything on it responds. Press 1, 2 or 3 to jump between them.

WorkbenchThe desktop as a living cell. Work happens in the nucleus, and the cells around it stretch, gather and feed it as Copilot works.
MosaicA living grid that grows out from a horizon. Copilot suggests tiles, fills empty slots and builds whole rows on request.
PinboardThe desktop as decorated art. Every widget is a sticker you can move, and Copilot restyles the board in one sentence.
Exploration, not product. Content is made up.
